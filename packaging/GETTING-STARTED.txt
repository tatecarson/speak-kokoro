Windows and Linux: open Speak Kokoro, select text in your document, then click Read selection on the floating toolbar. No manual copy or paste is needed. Use Pause or Stop while keeping your document in view. Settings & text opens the optional pasted-text reader; closing it returns to the toolbar. Close the toolbar to quit.

Selection support: Windows apps must expose text through UI Automation. Linux uses PRIMARY selection through xclip on X11 or wl-clipboard on supported Wayland desktops. If selection is unavailable, the toolbar explains it; it never reads old clipboard contents instead.

Speak Kokoro

Python, the speech model, voices, and pronunciation data are included.
No separate Python or eSpeak installation is required.

Windows: open "Speak Kokoro" from the Start menu.
Mac: open Speak Kokoro from Applications for the menu bar and reading panel.
Linux: open "Speak Kokoro" from your applications menu.

In the optional Settings & text window, paste or type text and choose Read aloud.
Choose a voice and speed below the text. Pause, resume, go back a sentence,
skip ahead, or stop with the playback buttons. Preferences save automatically.
Ctrl+Enter reads, Esc stops, and Ctrl+O opens a plain text file.
Closing the floating toolbar stops reading and unloads the model.

Optional commands: use "Speak Kokoro terminal" on Windows, or your normal
terminal on Mac/Linux. You can open the floating toolbar with:
  speak-kokoro --gui

Read a sentence:
  speak-kokoro "Hello! Kokoro is ready."

Stop reading:
  speak-kokoro --stop

Other controls:
  speak-kokoro --toggle
  speak-kokoro --next
  speak-kokoro --prev
  speak-kokoro --quit

Voice and speed:
  speak-kokoro --voice bm_george --speed 1.1 "Try another voice."
  speak-kokoro --voices

Check speech generation without playing audio:
  speak-kokoro --self-test
