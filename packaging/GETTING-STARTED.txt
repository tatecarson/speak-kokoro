Speak Kokoro

Python, the speech model, voices, and pronunciation data are included.
No separate Python or eSpeak installation is required.

Windows: open "Speak Kokoro terminal" from the Start menu.
Mac: open Speak Kokoro from Applications for the menu bar and reading panel.
Linux: open a terminal.

Read a sentence:
  speak-kokoro "Hello! Kokoro is ready."

Stop reading:
  speak-kokoro --stop

Other controls:
  speak-kokoro --toggle
  speak-kokoro --next
  speak-kokoro --prev
  speak-kokoro --quit

Voice and speed:
  speak-kokoro --voice bm_george --speed 1.1 "Try another voice."
  speak-kokoro --voices

Check speech generation without playing audio:
  speak-kokoro --self-test

Windows and Linux currently use terminal commands. A graphical interface will
be a separate update. Mac includes the existing menu bar and reading panel.

Help and documentation: https://github.com/tatecarson/speak-kokoro
Third-party licenses and source archives are included in the app's licenses folder.
